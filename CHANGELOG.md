## 0.3.0

- **New options panel.** Your headings and the datapoints under them now appear together in one list, in the order they appear on your tooltip.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag datapoints into headings, between headings to show them at heading level, into (Hidden), or back to the available list. Drag headings and spacers to reorder them. This replaces the Up / Down, Move Up / Move Down and Add to Heading buttons.
- **Follows TSM's tooltip settings.** Datapoints you switch off in TSM > Settings > Tooltip Settings no longer appear in the options panel, and are taken out of your layout. Switch one back on in TSM and it's in the available list, ready to place again. Renames and muting are kept.
- **New setting: Remember where datapoints switched off in TSM were placed** (off by default). Keeps a switched-off datapoint's place in your layout, so switching it back on in TSM puts it back where it was.
- **Hide button.** Hide a datapoint without dragging it to (Hidden). Unhide puts it back.
- New headings and spacers are added below the selected heading instead of at the end.
- Double-click a heading or datapoint to rename it.

## 0.2.0

- **Mute datapoints.** Select a datapoint and click **Mute** to show it in gray as secondary information, so the numbers you check most stand out. Click **Unmute** to bring back its normal colors. Muted datapoints keep that setting when you click Reset Headings.
